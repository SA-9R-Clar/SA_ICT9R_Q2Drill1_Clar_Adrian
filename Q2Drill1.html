A Guide to Cycling



Cycling as a sport is the competitive physical activity of riding pedal-driven, human-powered bicycles to go faster than opponents or finish a course in the shortest time. It is governed globally by the Union Cycliste Internationale (UCI) and features prominently in the Summer Olympic Games.

Basic Equipment

  • Bicycle
  • Pants
  • Shoes
  • Shirts

Basic Rules

  • Follow stoplights
  • Signal where you're going
  • Avoid the center of the roads
  • Always wear a helmet
  • Obey traffic signs

Types of Bicycles
